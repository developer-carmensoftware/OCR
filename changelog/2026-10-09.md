- **DB** (`a9256a81`, migration `20261009000000_pms_processing.sql`):
  - `pms_events` grows the email ledger's state: `status`, `review_payload` while pending, `jv_no`, the approve claim and the reviewer;
  - `pms_code_mappings`: per BU, keyed on the PMS code;
  - `pms_settings`: JV prefix, auto-post default off;
  - a `pms_interface` module row (no charge; needed for the LLM log's FK);
  - a `pms-process` pg_cron sweep every 10 minutes.
- **Processing** (`2ee77c34`, `services/pms/`):
  - **Trigger.** The webhook starts processing the day it stores. A repeat hook re-reads an unposted day.
  - **Read and map.**
    - The day is read back with the BU's stored Carmen credential.
    - Codes map to the BU's saved rules; the LLM runs only for codes never approved.
    - Balance = signed sum, Guest Ledger reversed.
  - **Park or post.**
    - A day parks with the email queue's flags, or posts when it is clean and the BU has auto-post and a prefix.
    - Posting is provisionally `/gljv` (CA-116 is open).
    - A failed read is retried three times.
- **Review API** (`7d934b94`):
  - the day's detail;
  - approve: saves the new codes' accounts, rebuilds the JV server-side and posts it, then re-checks the BU's other parked days;
  - reject;
  - the posting settings.
- **Queue** (`81607ed4`):
