## Backend (branch `fix/pms-wake-on-token`, not pushed)

`16:47` **A token stored from the email settings now wakes PMS days.**
- **The bug.** Only `PUT /pms/credential` woke days stopped by `no_credential` / `carmen_unauthorized`. A token refreshed any other way left a day that had spent its 3 attempts at `failed` for good: from the email settings (the queue's Reconnect button leads there), or re-proven by the daily check.
- **The fix.** The sweep wakes those days whenever `carmen_token_verified_at` is newer than the day's last attempt (`_wake_for_new_credential` in `services/pms/process.py`).
- **Tests.** Two new unit tests. The SQL was proven on the dev DB in a rolled-back transaction, against `carmencloud`'s real stalled day: 0 days woken with the `09:15` verification, 1 after a fresh one, and the row unchanged after the rollback.
