## PMS: a day is one row whatever its PMS name's casing (decision-log #58, branch `fix/pms-hook-casing`)

`14:25` **The bug** (suite 3, F-03/W-03): a hook spelled `HotelTime`, after the 06:00 sweep had
stored the day as Carmen's list spells it (`Hoteltime`), stored a second day. The queue listed
12/11/2026 twice.
- The sweep's own check ignored case; the hook's insert conflicted only on the exact `event_id`.

**Backend**
- `process.store_day`, which both the hook and the sweep store through, skips the insert when
  the BU holds the day in any casing (`INSERT … SELECT … WHERE NOT EXISTS (lower(event_id) = …)`).
- The hook's repeat path finds that row by `lower(event_id)`.
- No migration.
