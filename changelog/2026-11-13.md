# 2026-11-13

## PMS: the AI's forbidden pair keeps its account; blank rows are asked again (decision-log #57, branch `feat/pms-ai-every-row`)

`10:25` **The problem.** On QA carmencloud, three parked days had 31 new rows with no AI pick
(Cloudbeds 28 of 78, HotelTime 2 of 35, Comanche 1 of 21).
- **Every one was an answer the AI gave and we threw away.** It paired department 103 with a
  balance-sheet account 103's `DefaultAccount` list does not allow, and `_validate_codes`
  dropped the account.
- **A day whose AI call failed outright parked with no picks**, and nothing asked again.

**Backend**
- **`mapping.suggest` repairs a forbidden pair** (`_home_dept`): the account moves to the
  department this BU's own Carmen table uses most with it, else GEN if GEN allows it, else it
  is dropped as before.
- **Rows still blank are asked once more, at once**, alone, with why their answer was refused
  (and what the department named allows). One extra call, only when blanks remain.
- **The sweep asks again** (`process._resuggest_blank_days`, before the requeue):
  - for every parked day's blank rows, hourly, at most 3 times (`ai_retries`/`ai_at` in the
    payload), against Carmen's table as it is now;
  - a try counts even when it fails;
  - a day being approved is left alone.
- **`with_carmen_keys`** asks again for a pick with no account, and takes `carmen_blanks=False`
  for the retry.
- **Unchanged:**
  - Credit Card's `_validate_codes`;
  - the UI;
  - an AI pick still waits for a person, so nothing posts.
- **After deploy, days already parked are asked again in the first sweep** (they have no
  `ai_retries` yet), QA's test days included.
