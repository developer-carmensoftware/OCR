## `12:21` — Subscription-cycle billing model (uncommitted, in progress)

Turned billing from a one-off credit top-up into a real subscription with a monthly cycle. Aligns the system with the intended 3-stage lifecycle (request → slip/review → approve → active cycle). Policy = **Option A** (renew only after the current cycle lapses), with seams to switch to queued/replace-now later.
