## `15:10` — Pricing: monthly / annual subscriptions + license window −1 day (uncommitted)

Added an **Annual** billing option to `#/pricing` alongside Monthly. Annual = **10% off** (pay
`monthly × 12 × 0.9`); the doc allowance is unchanged (same per-month quota, **resets each month**)
— only the license length (1 year) and the price differ. Cards in annual mode show the **discounted
per-month price** as the hero, the original monthly price struck through, and the yearly total as a
caption (e.g. `฿2,241/mo · ฿26,892/yr · Save 10%`). The toggle is **Annual (save 10%) | Monthly**.
Separately, license windows now end one day early so consecutive periods tile without overlapping.
