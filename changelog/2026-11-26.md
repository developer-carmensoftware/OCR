- `09:30` **Frontend (`JvEditor.tsx`, review dialog):** the JV debit/credit amount field reformatted its own text on every keystroke, so selecting an amount and typing `1200` left `1.00` (the first key was reformatted under the caret), Backspace at the end of `1,000.00` did nothing, and clearing the field made the leg 0, dropped its row and took the input with it.
