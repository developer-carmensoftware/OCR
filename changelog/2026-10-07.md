`16:10` **CA-113: at most 2 active PMS keys per BU**
- `api_keys.issue()` counts the tenant's unrevoked keys and refuses a third with 409:
  "This business unit already has 2 active keys. Revoke one before creating another."
  Two is enough for a rotation (new key + old key while Carmen swaps).
- The rule lives in the service, so it holds for admin and for the coming BU page (CA-117).
  The admin create dialog shows the refusal in its error slot.
- The race is noted in a `ponytail:` comment: count-then-insert lets two creates in the same
  instant make a third key.
