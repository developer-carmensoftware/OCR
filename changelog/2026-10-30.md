## Security hardening, round 2 — PR 2: retention (branch `feat/retention-mailbox-filenames`, stacked on PR 1, not merged)

`10:20` Decision-log #50, PR 2.

- **Ingest mailbox keeps 14 days.** `imap.purge_expired`:
  - moves every message that arrived `BEFORE since_arg()` to the Trash. That is the exact
    complement of the poll's `SINCE`, so nothing a poll could still pick up is touched;
  - finds the `\Trash` folder through `LIST`, then `UID MOVE` in chunks of 100. Fallbacks:
    COPY + `\Deleted` + EXPUNGE without MOVE, and a plain delete with no Trash;
  - runs as `POST /api/v1/carmen/email-ingest/purge` (job token; `?dry_run=true` counts only),
    scheduled daily at 04:00 UTC by migration `20261030000100`.
  - Found on the real dev mailbox: imaplib's pre-login `capabilities` lacks MOVE on Gmail. The
    code asks `CAPABILITY` after login.
- **Filenames and error text kept 12 months** (migration `20261030000200`).
  `fn_scrub_old_filenames()` sets `ocr_tasks.original_filename='scrubbed'` and nulls its
  `error_message`, nulls `ap_invoices.original_filename`, and nulls
  `credit_ledger.ref` on `consumption`/`refund` rows. Rows, counts and amounts stay. Daily at
  04:20 UTC.
- **Tests:**

  - local Supabase: both migrations apply, pgTAP still 2/2;
  - `test_retention_scrub.py` (real DB) 2/2. It fails against a no-op function.
- **E2E `scripts/qa/retention_ui.mjs` 5/5** on the local stack:

  - the admin ledger keeps the charge without its filename;

    window, nothing moved;
  - no page errors.
