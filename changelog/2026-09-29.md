*after* the dropdown changes. CA-95's `useSettlementMapping` loads once, when the bank changes.

again, and a saved KBANK mapping showed as blank rows.

  bank. It is still not re-seeded by edits elsewhere on the page. `useMapping` exposes
  `mappingsBankCode`.
- `useBankConfig`'s first fetch is scoped to the bank the dropdown opens on. `?bank=` wins

  blanks over the real mapping. Seeds are now tagged, and they keep any dept/acc this bank
  already has for the same code, which also recovers rows saved untagged before this fix.
