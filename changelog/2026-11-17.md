## PMS: a day closed while it is processed stays closed (decision-log #59, branch `fix/pms-closed-while-processing`)

`10:30` **Suite 2 (X-03, 2026-11-13) caught a race.**
- `switch_off` closed a day while a worker was reading it (Carmen's preview and the AI take
  about 20 s with real data).
- The worker's park then wrote the day back to `pending_review`.
- `_write` updated by id, whatever the day had become.

**Backend (`services/pms/process.py`)**
- **`_write` lands only while the day is still `received`,** and says whether it did. When it does
  not, the result is dropped and logged, and the outcome reads `closed`.
- **A post Carmen made is always recorded** (`open_only=False`).
- **The key is read again just before `post_day`** (`_switched_off`, the same close as at the
  start).
