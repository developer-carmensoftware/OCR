## `22:45` LLM routing evidence & provenance — make the no-train claim auditable

Answering the sharp customer question "how much can you actually PROVE no-train?":
no-train is a 3-link chain — (1) we send the `deny` directive → provable, (2) the
request routes to an approved provider → observable, (3) Google doesn't train →
contractual, not technically provable by anyone. This phase captures links 1–2 as
auditable evidence and reframes the whitepaper to match. Records only the directive
and resolved-provider name per call — never request bodies/images (no-storage preserved).
