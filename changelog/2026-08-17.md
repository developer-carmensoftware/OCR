### Frontend

`10:40` **Tutorial highlight always has rounded corners.** The ring copies its target's
`border-radius`, so steps pointing at square-cornered targets (the two `<section
class="pricing-section">` blocks, `.checkout-grid`, `.pf-toolbar` — steps 4–8 of the
purchase tour) drew a hard rectangle. `TutorialModal.tsx` now floors it:
`Math.max(radius * view.scale, MIN_RADIUS)` with `MIN_RADIUS = 12` stage px. Already-rounded
targets (the pill Buy button, `.panel-card`, the pending-order banner) are unaffected. The
floor is in stage px, like the ring itself, so the corner keeps the same softness at any zoom.
