## PMS: the PartnerApi plan (branch `docs/pms-partnerapi-dry-run`)
- `07:50` **Decision-log #52 (proposed).** A `PartnerApi` PMS puts no day in the Data Bank:
  Carmen fetches, converts and posts in one step, so there is nothing to park or map first. A
  raw GET from the PMS would not help, because converting it is Carmen's job.
  - **What we ask Carmen:** a **dry-run of `interfacePostGL`** (ask L) that answers the
    converted rows without posting or storing.
  - **What we build:** our `pms-process` sweep opens yesterday's day per BU × PMS with a live
    key. Processing takes the rows from the dry-run instead of the Data Bank, and the rest is
    unchanged: the AI, the queue, Approve, auto-post.
  - **Decided by the user:** the dry-run, our cron, and posting as for CarmenApi.
  - **Docs:** `07-status.md` ask L, `PMS_INTEGRATION.md` §7 item 7 (the spec for Carmen), and
    a `02-architecture.md` note. Nothing is built yet.
- `08:02` #52, on the user's question "100 BUs = 100 dry-runs at once?": no.
  - **Why not:** the opener only INSERTs the days and does not `kick` them, and the sweep
    processes them one at a time (≤ 50 per run, locked).
  - **Recorded:** the load per BU, and two ceilings — throughput past a few hundred BUs, and
    the hook path's uncapped `kick`, which needs the same fixed concurrency.
