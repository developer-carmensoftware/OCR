## Backend (PR #288, branch `feat/carmen-ai-status`)

`09:54` **`GET /api/v1/carmen/ai-status`: which AI sales popup Carmen shows a BU** (`b1a6cb6a`)

- **Public by request, no auth.** Carmen passes the SSO link's `uri` + `bu` and gets back only
  `{"stage": "never_used" | "trial" | "near_limit" | "exhausted" | "paid"}`. Carmen owns the copy,
  the price and the CTA; `paid` (any plan or top-up) shows nothing; any non-200 shows nothing.
- **Accepted risk:** anyone who guesses a (host, bu) learns its stage, `paid` included. What the
  hardening bounds is speed and DB load, not disclosure:
  - an unknown (host, bu) reads `never_used`, so the endpoint cannot tell which BUs exist;
  - a 300/min ceiling on the endpoint as a whole on top of the middleware's 120/min/IP;
  - `Cache-Control: max-age=300`;
  - read-only: unlike `/auth/exchange` it creates no tenant, grant or session.
- Stage rule `ai_stage()` in `services/shared/credits.py`: trial usage = 30 − balance, near limit
  from 25 used (`NEAR_LIMIT_USED`).
- **Not done here:** `TRUST_PROXY=true` + `TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS=1` on the QA backend (`-xntb`) is a
  Render dashboard change; until then every QA client shares one 120/min bucket. No release note
  (nothing changes in our app) and no VERSION bump (batched per release).
