# 2026-08-28

## Email Automation — human-in-the-loop review (in progress, branch `feat/email-hitl-review`)

Design settled and written up in
[`docs/email-automation/07-human-in-the-loop.md`](../docs/email-automation/07-human-in-the-loop.md).
Email ingest currently posts to Carmen with nobody in between — the right shape for a pipeline
people already trust, the wrong one for a BU switching it on for the first time. A document now
parks at `pending_review` until someone approves it, and the BU turns that off with a per-BU
`auto_post` switch once it has seen enough. The Credit Card module's landing page becomes the
review queue; the wizard moves to its own route.

### Uncommitted / in progress

### DB

`09:55` **`20260828000000_email_review_queue.sql`** — `email_documents` gains
`review_payload jsonb`, `reviewed_by varchar(36)`, `reviewed_at timestamptz`; a partial index
on `(tenant_id, created_at desc) where status = 'pending_review'`; `email_ingest_settings` gains
`auto_post boolean not null default false`.

- **`review_payload` narrows a documented rule rather than repealing it.** `CLAUDE.md` and
  `04-data-model.md` both say extracted line items are never persisted. They now say: not
  persisted **after a document is resolved**. A document waiting on a human keeps its payload
  because the only alternative is a second vision call the customer pays for twice; `_finish`
  clears the column to `NULL` on every terminal transition.
- **The built JV rows are deliberately not in the payload.** The review screen derives them live
  from `details` against the *current* accounting config, the way `AccountingReview` already
  does — a reviewer must see what would post now, not what would have posted at extract time.
- **Neither is the Carmen credential.** Token and URI are re-read at approve time; they rotate,
  and `sweep_token_health` may have unverified them while the document sat.
- `auto_post` defaults `false`, so every BU with the feature on wakes up in review mode. That is
  the direction you can walk back from. Its known sharp edge — `PUT /settings` is a full replace,
  so an older Carmen client omits the field and silently resets it — is accepted and recorded in
  the migration with its upgrade path.
