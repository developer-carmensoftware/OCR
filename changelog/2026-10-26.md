## Backend + Frontend (branch `feat/ca-123-ap-vendor-rules`)

`11:15` **CA-123: AP Invoice reads Polar Bear's invoice off its PACK columns** (vendor reading rules by tax ID)
- **The bug, from a customer's Carmen (AP Seq 16462, entered 15/10).** Polar Bear Mission's invoice (Freshket's layout) prints two qty/price sets per row. It was read off UNIT DETAILS, so coconut milk came out as 12 × 76.32 and flour as 10 × 38.22. Those unit prices are Amount ÷ unit qty, rounded, so they do not multiply back. Carmen recomputes Total from Qty × Price, so its Total (4,211.91) disagreed with Unpaid (4,211.97) and with the document (4,212.01).
- **`llm/prompts/ap_invoice.py` `VENDOR_RULES`:** `tax ID → Thai instruction`, appended to the AP prompt as rule 8, the same for every BU. It holds one vendor, `0105559082715`, whose rule is to read qty/price from PACK DETAILS only. The rule fires when the tax ID appears anywhere on the page, not on the model's own `vendorTaxId` read: that read happens in the same call, and on this invoice it has come back as the buyer. Still one LLM call. The `ponytail:` note gives the ceiling: move the rules to a table + admin page past a handful of vendors.
- **`postprocess.py` `apQtyPriceMismatch` warning:** fires for a vendor on the list whose undiscounted rows still don't multiply to their Amount (more than 1 satang off). It names the lines, shows in the existing Step 3 amber banner, and does not block. EN/TH copy is in `i18n/dict/warn.ts`.
- **Measured before the change.** The current prompt on this sample read PACK 6/6 (`gemini-3.1-flash-lite-preview`, QA's model) and 4/4 (`gemini-2.5-flash-lite`, `render.yaml`'s prod value). The bug did not reproduce locally. But 2.5 read the buyer (`0105560215995`) as the vendor 4/4, and the reporter's own scan on dev (10-19 14:38, BU `carmen`) got the vendor wrong too.
- **After the change.** 3.1 read PACK 5/5 and 2.5 read PACK 3/3, and 2.5 now reads the right vendor tax ID 3/3. Old vs new prompt on 10 other AP documents (demo, CA-122 QA set, 2 real PDFs, 3 phone photos): **10/10 identical** qty, price, line amount and vendor tax ID.
