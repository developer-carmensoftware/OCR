# 2026-07-31

### `10:15` — Email Automation v2: integration contract for the Carmen team

New requirements replace the `feat/email-flow` pilot: settings move to Carmen's screens,
Carmen gets notified by webhook, documents post to the JV **without human approval**, the
email-verification step is dropped, and **both auto-forward and manual forward** must work.
Fresh branch `feat/email-automation-v2` off `main`; `feat/email-flow` stays as reference
and will not be merged.

First deliverable is the contract, because two of the four open decisions can only be
answered by the Carmen team and everything else waits on them.

**Docs** — `docs/CARMEN_INTEGRATION.md` (new)

- Part A: settings API (Carmen → OCR). Tenant stays the existing `(host, bu)` pair from
  `/auth/exchange`, so no new identity concept. Rule list is replace-not-merge;
  `pdf_password` is write-only (`has_password` on read).
- Part B: webhook envelope, HMAC-SHA256 signing over `"{timestamp}.{raw_body}"`,
  at-least-once with dedupe on `id`, 24h backoff. Events: `notification.created`,
  `subscription.activated` / `subscription.lapsed`, plus a proposed `document.posted` /
  `document.failed` pair — with no approval step those are the only outcome signal.
- Part C: the JV-posting credential problem stated with three options.
  `post_gljv()` uses the caller's 30-minute Carmen session token, which a scheduled job
  does not have.
- Tax ID documented as a **hard prerequisite** for switching the feature on: 13 digits,
  check-digit validated at write time, globally unique across BUs, requested from Carmen's
  company master rather than typed by the user.
- `subscription.lapsed` called out explicitly — without it Carmen would leave Email
  Automation switched on after a package ends.
- Open items marked **OPEN** with a checklist of the six things we need back (§5).

**Manual forward support (§0.1)** — the pilot was auto-forward only. Forwarding by hand
breaks all three signals it relied on, so the contract records how each is replaced:

- Issuing bank moves from *sender address* → *detected from the document*, so rules are now
  keyed on `bank_code` and `bank_sender_email` becomes an optional fast path. A protected
  PDF is opened by trying the BU's own rule passwords when the bank is not yet known.
- The bank's DKIM signature does not survive a manual forward, which makes the tax-ID check
  the primary ownership control rather than a secondary one.
- Nothing identifies the BU except the address the mail was sent to — which effectively
  settles the ingest-address question in favour of a per-BU alias (§2.5); a shared mailbox
  would require registering every employee who might forward.
- The same document arriving twice (auto + by hand) has two different Message-IDs, so it is
  caught by the existing document-number duplicate guard, with the credit refunded.

Not yet started: ingestion core port, TIN gate, server-side JV builder, outbox.
