## PMS: AI suggestions no longer lost to a missing brace (same branch)

`14:17` The E2E run turned up a problem: one PMS day in three parked with no AI picks.

  the object's closing `}` (3 of 5 HotelTime answers, `finish_reason` "stop"). The old prompt did
